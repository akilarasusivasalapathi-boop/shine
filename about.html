About Us

Built to give textile teams a straight answer.

Shine Testing Services India (OPC) Pvt Ltd is a textile testing, inspection, and quality support service based in Tirupur, Tamil Nadu — working with brands, exporters, manufacturers, and sourcing teams across the categories where quality decisions carry real cost.

Based in Tirupur, Tamil Nadu, India

Who We Are

A service partner, not a report factory.

Plenty of organisations can run a test. Fewer will tell you which test you actually need, what the result means for your product, and what to do when it comes back marginal.

SHINE was built around that gap. We coordinate the testing and inspection work, and we stay in the conversation through interpretation and corrective direction — because a number without context rarely resolves anything.

Tirupur sits at the centre of one of India's most concentrated textile clusters. Being based here means we understand the production realities our clients are working inside, not just the specification on the page.

What We Stand For

Four commitments that shape how we work.

Clear Direction

You should always know what is being tested, why it matters, and what happens next. No ambiguity about scope or outcome.

Dependable Execution

Committed timelines that hold, and work carried out to the method actually specified — not an approximation of it.

Practical Guidance

Technical support in language your sourcing and production teams can act on immediately, without translation.

Confidentiality

Product information, test data, and commercial detail stay between us and you. Always, and without needing to be asked.

Our Approach

Scope it properly once, and the rest gets easier.

Most quality problems we see did not begin as testing problems. They began as scoping problems — the wrong method set, testing at the wrong stage, or a specification nobody had fully read.

  • We start by understanding the product and the market it is going to
  • We shortlist the methods that carry real risk, not the longest available list
  • We test at the stage where a finding can still change the outcome
  • We report in a form you can hand straight to a supplier or a customer
  • We stay available after the report for the questions it raises

Work With Us

Tell us about your product and we will tell you what it needs.

The first conversation is a scoping conversation. Send the category, the requirement, and the timeline, and we will come back with a plan.
